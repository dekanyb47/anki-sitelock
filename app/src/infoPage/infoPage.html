Anki SiteLock Information

What is this extension for?

It was made to help encourage users to study in Anki (especially those who struggle with consistency, or those who have a lot of reviews piled up), and cut down on their social media usage at the same time.

How does it work?

It works by locking specified websites, and only allowing you to unlock them for a certain amount of time if you have enough credits. To earn credits, you need to study cards Anki, and the extension will check it automatically. Each unique card studied is equal to one credit, and this includes reviews, as well as new cards.

How do I start using the extension?

Step 1: Download Anki version 2.1.x or newer.
Step 2: Download the newest version of AnkiConnect via AnkiWeb. You can do that in Anki by going to Tools -> Add-ons -> Get Add-ons, and pasting in the code '2055492159'.
Step 3: Setup the variables inside the extension's settings. In the top right corner in firefox, click on Extensions, then Anki SiteLock. From there, you can access the settings.
